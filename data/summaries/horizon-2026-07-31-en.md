## [MLVC: Multi-platform Learned Video Codec for Real-World Deployment](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vb3xwd/mlvc_multiplatform_learned_video_codec_for/) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A new learned video codec named MLVC is introduced, designed to solve cross-platform compatibility issues that have prevented neural video codecs from being deployed in the real world. 这项工作直接攻克了AI视频压缩实际应用的一大障碍，有可能使更高效的学习型编解码器在各种应用中取代H.264和AV1等传统标准。 The codec achieves real-time performance of about 100 FPS for 360p/540p video on consumer NPUs from Apple, Intel, and Qualcomm, and the author is actively engaging with the community to answer questions.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/tanelai · Jul 30, 19:40

**Background**: Traditional hand-engineered video codecs like H.264, H.265, and AV1 still dominate because they have ubiquitous hardware acceleration, making them power-efficient. Neural video codecs, while promising, face significant hurdles: they are often large and power-hungry, and suffer from critical cross-platform compatibility issues where minor numerical differences between different NPUs can break the entropy decoding process, making the video stream unplayable. MLVC proposes a solution by transmitting the entropy-model scale parameters through the hyperprior, so the network does not need to run bit-exactly on different hardware.
